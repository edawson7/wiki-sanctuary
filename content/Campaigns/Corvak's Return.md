*Corvak's Return*, often abbreviated to CR, was a mini-campaign set in the world of Sanctuary. The first session of this campaign was held in late 2025. This campaign lasted about 20 sessions over the course of about 7 months.
### Chronological History
Within the world of Sanctuary, *Corvak's Return* takes place during Welsca of 1073, 11 years after *[[Crack in Sanctuary]]*, and 15 years prior to the events of *[[Sanctuary Beyond the Veil|Sanctuary: Beyond the Veil]]*.
