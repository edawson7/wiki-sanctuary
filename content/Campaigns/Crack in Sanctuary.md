*Crack in Sanctuary*, often abbreviated to CiS, was the first campaign to take place within Sanctuary, as it was the setting's debut. The first session of this campaign was in late April 2021, as the second session was on May 1st, 2021. The campaign lasted a total of 30 sessions over the course of 13 months, with the last session being held on June 11th, 2022.

Before being called '*Crack in Sanctuary*', it was initially called '*Crack in Vuarscrya'*. The name of the campaign shifted after [[The Makevens]] had their first fight against [[The Casualty (Malthu)]], as the [[The Lanterns of Sanctuary|Lantern]] of [[Vuarscrya]] was shattered. That event alone shifted the scope of the campaign from just the continent of Vuarscrya to the entirety of [[The Isles of Sancturn|Sanctuary]]. 
### Chronological History
Within the world of Sanctuary, *Crack in Sanctuary* takes place in autumn of 1062, before the events of both *[[Corvak's Return]]* and *[[Sanctuary Beyond the Veil|Sanctuary: Beyond the Veil]]*.
