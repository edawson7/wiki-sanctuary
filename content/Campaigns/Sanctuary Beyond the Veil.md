*Sanctuary: Beyond the Veil*, often abbreviated to S:BtV, was the second campaign set in the world of Sanctuary. The first session of this campaign was held on September 9th, 2022, three months after the end of *[[Crack in Sanctuary]]*. This campaign lasted 57 sessions over the course of 31 months, with the epilogue being held on April 25th, 2025.
### Chronological History
Within the world of Sanctuary, *Sanctuary: Beyond the Veil* takes place within the first half of 1087, after the events of both *Crack in Sanctuary* and *[[Corvak's Return]]*.
