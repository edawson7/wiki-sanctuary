Aercasa, known as Aercasa the Prophet, was a Golden Dragonborn that served as a cleric of [[Feynaria]]. She is known for her effort in [[The Rightful Valor]], and close relationship with [[The Casualty (Malthu)|Malthu]] before their resurgence as The Casualty.
### Early Life
Aercasa was born on [[Daughter Island]] of [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|Ilkon]] sometime in the year 1031. She claimed poachers killed her family during a raid on their tribe sometime in her teens, as she was the sole survivor. In her recollections, she stated that she had wandered the abandoned villages and great plains of Daughter Island until 1049, when she came across a statue of a woman with three eyes. Starving and desperate, Aercasa prayed to this Goddess, betraying her hereditary faith in [[Soliana]] passed on from her Golden Dragonborn ancestors. In that moment, [[Feynaria]] revealed herself to Aercasa, before teleporting her to a city in mainland Ilkon and gifting her a thousand platinum coins. 

Aercasa was given the ability to see the near future, a sense of precognition. She would often use this ability to earn trust in others and instill faith in Feynaria across those who she meets along her path. She quickly became the head bishop of a religion nobody had heard of before, and thousands travelled to meet Aercasa, joining her new church. 
### Time in The Rightful Valor
In 1053, when Aercasa was 22, she came across Malthu for the first time. He had sought her out while she was touring [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla|Bivoulla]], and offered to be a means to her power, a chance to claim valor. He told her about [[The Lanterns of Sanctuary]] and his plan to destroy them. Hesitant at first, Aercasa was coaxed into following Malthu by Feynaria. From that moment forward, Aercasa had lost herself, becoming a puppet for both Malthu and Feynaria. Feynaria often used Aercasa as a vessel, informing Malthu of updates across the world, scrying on potential adversaries, and opening portals for them to travel through. 

Feynaria met her fateful end in a battle against The Makevens, where Calthor Igniski called upon Soliana for a divine intervention in battle. Soliana answered his prayer, and in an ironic turn of fate, summoned an Alternate Aercasa, only to instantly collide into and annihilate Aercasa. Outside of a little bit of splattered blood, there was no way to physically prove that Aercasa had existed. Her death is commonly attributed to the death of the last Golden Dragonborn, marking the race itself as extinct.
