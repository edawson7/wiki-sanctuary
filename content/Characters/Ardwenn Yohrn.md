Ardwenn Yohrn is a Silver Dragon, most often disguised as an older Elven man, and is the Dean at [[The College of Apallyon]]. He is a benevolent influence not only on the College and [[The Kingdom of Apallyon|Apallyon]], but also on the entirety of [[The Isles of Sancturn|Sanctuary]]. Beyond his goodwill, Yohrn is also characterized by his rational, by-the-book mindset.
### Early Life
Ardwenn Yohrn was born in 767 somewhere within Apallyon's southwestern mountains, and was named Ardwenn, meaning "piercing brilliance" in draconic. His mother would tell him to stay away from humanoids, as they were often too violent, and would deceive to get their way. Despite this, she would often invite a friend of her late partner, [[Var Oprix]], to visit her and Ardwenn. He would act as a father figure for Ardwenn in his adolescence, and taught him important lessons, setting the groundwork for Ardwenn's rational personality. When Ardwenn turned 34 years old, his mother passed away from a disease that had afflicted her for the past five decades. She was just over 400 years of age when she perished. 

After his mother passed, Ardwenn quickly became lonely, and decided to search for Var Oprix within civilization. He disguised himself as a non-descript human, and walked the streets of [[Avalon]], searching and asking about a certain 'Var Oprix'. After getting distracted with the delicious local cuisine, and the Feystay festivities occurring, he found a lead. An Elven student by the name of Liam Daven noticed who he was asking about, and informed him that Var Oprix is the Dean at the College of Apallyon. He also reckoned that Ardwenn seemed pretty intelligent, and should attend classes there. The two became fast friends, and after a week of travelling, which Ardwenn had used to learn many humanoid practices, they arrived at the college.

When Ardwenn arrived, he sought out Dean Oprix, hoping to get an audience with him in order to reveal who he truly was. Ardwenn took some placement assessments, and scored extremely high on many of them, earning himself the audience he sought. When he met Var Oprix for the first time, Ardwenn spoke draconic, asking "Is it really you, Oprix, behind that Elven facade?" Var Oprix responded in his native tongue, asking "I could ask the same for you, my boy." Ardwenn told Oprix of his mother's passing, and asked if he could stay here and learn along with the other humanoids. Oprix obliged, stating that he shouldn't reveal his draconic nature to anyone, and he most adopt a surname. Oprix found "Yohrn" to be fitting, an oxymoronic portmanteau that combined the draconic words for gold and silver. It was also Oprix's idea to disguise oneself as an Elf, as they live so long, and are very common around Vuarscrya. 
### Life at The College of Apallyon
Yohrn earned a Magister Magnus, the highest degree of education that the College could award, as he studied for an allotted twenty years. He mostly studied in history, linguistics, and arcane sciences, with his favorite subject being Historiography. Throughout these years he remained within the confides of Apallyon, and scarcely returned to his true form. For a time, he had fell into the illusion himself, forgetting the secret he only shared with his mentor.

When he had graduated, Var Oprix gifted him a relic, one from before modern recorded history. It was a pentacle made of a metal alloy that nobody could identify. This relic was the beginning of Yohrn's hoard, not one made of gold, but one made up of ancient relics.
