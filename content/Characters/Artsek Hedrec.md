Artsek Hedrec, born Brosca 13th of 1013, is a Tiefling Druid under the Circle of the Eclipse. He was a member of [[The Rightful Valor]], and assisted [[The Makevens]] with their endeavor to defeat [[The Casualty (Malthu)]]. 
### Early Life
Artsek was born in a small rural town in [[The Kingdom of Ausonate|Ausonate]] named Grenton Village, about a days walk north of Art. He and his younger brother Hurlek, which was born four years later, were abandoned by their parents when he was nine years old. Forced with no other decision, Artsek raised himself and his brother in their little village.

As Artsek and Hurlek became teenagers, their distinct personalities became apparent. Artsek spent much of his free time in the woods, looking up at the stars, and tattooing his arms with a star map. Hurlek, on the other hand, engaged in mischievous activities, often partaking in theft in the late hours. 

One night in Autumn of 1034, Artsek returned home after a day trip to Art. He often used his wild-shaping ability to travel long distances as a bird, and today was no different. As Artsek was about to enter the home, he noticed the door was slightly opened. He entered in carefully, and noticed the sound of liquid dripping. As he produced a small flame in his hand to better see, he saw the figure of Hurlek holding a bloodied dagger, with the corpse of householder who had provided lodging to both him and Hurlek for the past five years. Behind them, the householder’s wife was hanging from the rafters, the blood drained from her body. Artsek was stunned, and stood there as Hurlek dropped the knife, and fled the scene, turning into a shadow in the night.

Before Artsek could think, guards arrived, and saw the scene, arresting Artsek in that moment. He typically had his brother’s back, and would even cover for some of his petty crimes, so Artsek’s cry for the truth, stating that Hurlek did this, went uncared for. Artsek would then spend the next 6 years in prison, before eventually being released due to his good behavior and persistent claims that he didn’t commit the crime.

Furious at his brother, and scared of what made him commit such acts, Artsek travelled across [[Vuarscrya]] searching for Hurlek. He needed to confront him, and possibly put an end to him. Artsek came across and worked with a group of adventurers called [[The Greenwood Blade]]. He often asked them to keep an eye out, and they promised they would.
### The Creation of Arthur
In 1049, [[Crimwood Holo]] of The Greenwood Blade was targeted by Hurlek’s bloodlust. While Crimwood was on an adventure on Pillarsent Island, Artsek noticed light coming from his house. He decided to investigate, and similarly to the events 15 years ago, found Crimwoods’s family slaughtered like cattle. However, Artsek didn’t freeze in his spot, and rather took the opportunity to attack Hurlek. He plunged a sword deep into Hurlek’s chest, slicing open a grave wound before casting a spell that incinerated his internal organs. Artsek had no pity, and showed no mercy as he saw his brother’s lack of change. Hurlek’s body dropped to the ground, and Artsek immediately attempted to save Crimwood’s daughter. Unfortunately, she was too long gone, and Artsek couldn’t save any of them. He fell to his knees, and pushed his face into the ground, crying. He lost his brother, and his brother took so much from this world, and unfortunately for Artsek, he wasn’t finished. 

Hurlek’s soul sprouted from his body, wrapping itself around Artsek, possessing his older brother. Artsek attempted to fight it, but in such a distraught state, lost any control he had over his body. Hurlek commanded Artsek’s body, and would later call himself Arthur, a mix of the two brothers. *For disambiguation, Arthur refers to Hurlek in control over Artsek’s body, and Hurlek refers to simply his own soul.*

Arthur would continue to terrorize the people of Vuarscrya until in 1057 when Malthu recruited him for The Rightful Valor, honing his bloodlust. Throughout this period of possession by Hurlek, Artsek would occasionally attempt to regain control. This was easiest when Arthur was alone, so Arthur happily accepted Malthu’s recruitment.
### Life During Crack in Sanctuary (Campaign 1)
In 1062, Arthur helped his companions in The Rightful Valor assassinate King Elzon Leadfinger. Although he did not claim the killing blow, the fusion between his control over Artsek’s druidic magic and Hurlek’s domain over shadows, empowered [[Zariah Ra'Solvak|Z]]’s spiritual manifestation, allowing her to decapitate the late king.

After The Makevens broke [[Elzon Leadfinger's Curse]], Arthur was left to act as a substitute for the void of power in Ausonate while the rest of The Rightful Valor headed south to intervene on [[Tornic's Perfect War|Ravalon's civil war]]. This choice was inconsequential for a time, until Malthu successfully destroyed Vuarscrya’s Lantern. The sudden Incursion affected Arthur differently, giving Artsek the ample opportunity to separate himself from Hurlek’s control. He reached out to The Makevens, requesting their aid.

When The Makevens arrived to respond to Artsek’s call, they found him fighting a dark, evil spirit. Upon first impression, The Makevens were weary, since they had just been betrayed by Malthu and Aercasa, worried this may be a use. Adalynn, however, took a leap of faith, asking Artsek if he trusted her. He nodded and grabbed her hand as she banished him to a different plane. Within the next few moments, they defeated Hurlek, freeing Artsek from his indefinite haunting. Artsek, forever grateful and personally ashamed he was not able to deal with Hurlek himself.

Allied with The Makevens, Artsek travelled to Ravalon with them in order to rescue Z, which he believed had been left for dead by Malthu and Aercasa. As they travelled south, The Makevens stopped in Rowehallow. Artsek, just recently coming to terms with reality, refused to enter the village. The idea of facing Crimwood Holo after all these years, possessed by the murderer of his family was too much to process. The fact that thirteen years have passed was already enough. 

In Ravalon, Artsek was able to assist The Makevens with saving Z, as she had been tortured and maimed by interrogation efforts. Once she was secured, Artsek used his druidic magic to transport everyone back to Rolsa, where he took care of Z further

Artsek heavily practiced healing magic as Z recovered from her time in Ravalon, eventually being able to regenerate the parts of her body that had been removed during her interrogation. For a week, he nursed her back to health, regenerated appendages and all. When she awoke, Artsek informed her of everything that has transpired, and she hugged him. She then began insist on taking care of herself, but didn’t mind his company. In this time of respite, Z had confided in Artsek, telling him the story of her past, including her name, Zariah. Artsek promised to keep her secrets for as long as she meant to keep them herself.
