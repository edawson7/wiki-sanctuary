Diallo Theren, born on Eilsca 15th 968, is an Elven wizard and alumni of [[The College of Apallyon]]. Diallo was an active member of the Forces Against Calamity, and helped defeat [[The Calamity]]. 
### Early Life
Diallo was born to Elias and Sybille Theren, two High Elven alumni of the College of Apallyon; Elias studied archaeology, and Sybille studied arcane arts. Set to follow in their footsteps, Diallo enrolled and began learning the arcane arts himself in 1013. He was a star student, and was offered a place in an expedition to Kalvera by [[Everwood Holo|Professor Everwood Holo]] in 1043. He, and four other students ([[Elsa Jarrett]], [[Illona Von Wothian]], [[Avent Warlock]], and [[Broncho Lentway]]), were meant to visit [[Kalvera (The Feywild)|Kalvera]] over the course of one week which stood in place of a final exam. 
### Time in Kalvera
When the group was sent to Kalvera, there was a mishap which led Diallo to be separated from his group. Unsure of what happened, the three others searched for Diallo while completing the required tasks asked of them for their final. After a week, they returned home, and informed Professor Holo of the bad news.

Diallo had spent his time in the Feywild searching for his cohorts, and after about a month came across the Spring Court. After meeting with Sage, the Great Spring Faerie, Diallo reached an agreement that would allow him to stay within Sage's domain, so long as Diallo completed tasks asked to him.

After Diallo's second month in his unexpected exile in Kalvera, he came across a set of adventurers that seemed to have ties to the material plane, being [[Leo Rexen|Leo]], [[Sorvella Faxon|Sora]], [[Thyrhall]], and [[Ukullach]]. Leo, who delegated for the group, introduced himself to Diallo as 'John', claiming 'Leo' was a codename when the others referred to him as such. When Leo informed Diallo that they were travelling to the Court of the Sun, Diallo requested to join in hopes of searching there for his missing friends. Both Sage and Leo's group had no issues with him joining to witness the [[Trisaam Sol'dava]] in the Court of the Sun, so he travelled with them until they returned. There, Diallo prematurely met [[Auberin Thayer|Auberin]] of the Autumn Court, and failed to find any of his cohorts. Leo and his group offered to bring him back home after informing him of what year it is, but Diallo refused, wishing to continue to search for his long-lost friends.
