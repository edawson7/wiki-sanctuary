Elevana Hurlek, born on Varsca 43rd 1071, is a Tiefling druid that was involved in the War Against Calamity, on the side of the Forces Against Calamity.
### Appearance
Elevana is a Tiefling with Elvish features. Most notably, she has antler-like horns that sprout symmetrically from just beyond her hairline above her eyes, each about 8 inches in length. Her tan skin is speckled with large freckles that resemble the print of leopards, and she has short, pointed ears. Elevana has bright, hazel eyes, and dark burgundy hair. Elevana stands at about 5'6, or about 6'2 including the height of her horns, and weighs just over 140 lbs. She is a type of Tiefling that does not have a tail.
### Early Life
Born to [[Artsek Hedrec]] and [[Zariah Ra'Solvak]] on Varsca 43rd 1071, Elevana was an unplanned part of their lives, but a very welcomed one. She is the only child of Artsek and Zariah, and is considered royalty of the Ra'Solvak lineage. As Zariah's relations with her family were restored, Elevana would often see her Elven cousin, Anavelle Ra'Solvak, who is a year younger than her. The two have had a sort of rivalry, with Anavelle often calling Elevana a 'country bumpkin', and the Elevana calling Anavelle a 'spoiled city rat'. Anavelle often hung her title of being a princess over Elevana, which soured the taste of royalty to her. 

True to the nickname Anavelle had given her, Elevana was raised in the countryside to the southwest of [[Solvak]]. She did not have many friends, and loved being around her parents. She spent a lot of time within nature and exploring the outdoors, and with the help of her father, gained druidic powers. 

Her horns first grew in when she was seven years old, and perhaps due to her father's connection to nature, seemed to be made out of bone like antlers are. When they did, her parents held a gathering for family and friends to celebrate her and her growth. According to Zariah, Elevana had taunted a stag, attempting to spar with it and her newly grown horns. This was before she could Wild Shape, and was flung into the air, and thrown to the side. Artsek had to intervene, sparring with the stag himself as Zariah took Elevana away. She had broken her arm in the process, but didn't cry. Instead, as Zariah took her away, she yelled at the deer "Just you wait 'til these bad boys grow out, ya hear?"
