Illona Von Wothian is the Prime Minister of the Vuarscryan Union, which she was a major campaigner for. She was also the Chief Minister of [[The Kingdom of Ausonate|Ausonate]] from  until 1068.
### Early Life
On the 5th of Welsca 984, Illona Von Wothian was born to a wealthy elven family in [[Rolsa, Ausonate|Rolsa]]. Her paternal family have historically been involved within Ausonate's politics, especially due to her father's inherited ferrying business. 

In 1032 when Illona was 48 years old, she enrolled at [[The College of Apallyon|the College of Apallyon]] to study both politics, magic, and the intricacies between the two.

In her tenth year, Illona had signed up for an expedition to the Spring Court within [[Kalvera (The Feywild)|the Feywild]], along with three others: [[Broncho Lentway]], [[Elsa Jarrett]], [[Diallo Theren]], and [[Avent Warlock]]. Held by [[Everwood Holo|Professor Everwood Holo]], this expedition became infamous as Diallo in the expedition went missing. While in Kalvera, Illona took a position as leader for the remaining group, helping keep heads cool and avoiding any further problems. When they returned, Illona was the one that informed Holo about Diallo's disappearance, and took the blame.

In 1047, four years after her expedition, Illona completed her studies at the College of Apallyon, becoming a master in Political Sciences with a minor in Arcane Policy. Illona was determined to help fix the tyrannical rule in Ausonate, but was advised by her family and mentors to move to and find a position in [[The Kingdom of Apallyon|Apallyon's]] government. 
### Chief Minister
Against the advice she received from those who cared about her, Illona pursued a position in Ausonate's kingdom. Over the course of seven years, Illona made good relations to those in power, advancing her position and power in the kingdom, and noting areas of corruption. In 1056, she landed a position as Chief Minister of Ausonate.
