Karmagen, a member of [[The Makevens]], is an influential mage in Sanctuary. 

### Early Life
Born as Tirich Gilith on Welsca 42nd, 992, Karmagen grew up in northern [[The Republic of Hauvi|Hauvi]], near the marshy town of Narpozzo. Little is known about this stage of his life, but it is known that he had a wife and had two children with her: Ledger Gilith, born in 1012, and [[Selance Alkoth|Lucian Gilith]], born in 1013.

Karmagen disappeared in 1020 after leaving his family in search of medicine or cure for his wife's diminishing health. His wife passed away shortly after, leaving Ledger and Lucian to raise themselves.
### Life in the Shadowfell
When Karmagen disappeared around 1020, he actually had lost himself in the Shadowfell. Similarly to his early life, many of his goals or intentions are unclear. Karmagen eventually became an amnesiac, losing most of his memories to the Shadowfell. Through these hardships, Karmagen's mind had broken, but his tenacity glued it back together.
### Life as a Makeven
In 1062, after years of attempting to control his newfound powers, Karmagen was able to eventually open a portal long and stable enough for him to pass through, allowing him to travel back to the Material Plane. In that moment, a portal opened above a table in a tavern somewhere in rural [[The Kingdom of Ausonate|Ausonate]]. Coincidentally, that table was occupied by a group of people headed to [[Rolsa, Ausonate|Rolsa]] to investigate [[The Rightful Valor|The Rightful Valor's]] insurrection, being [[Adalynn Panascria]], [[Calthor Igniski]], [[Dante]], and [[Ramon Burk]]. Off of a whim, and perhaps consequence to his shattered mind, Karmagen chose to join them in their mission.

As part of The Makeven's adventures, the group entered the Shadowfell. There, Karmagen and the rest of the group fought a being called Cyaquiem and an alternate Karmagen.

One of the most significant events to occur in his time as a Makeven was when we learned that Selance Alkoth was his son, Lucian. This fact, which was realized near the same time that [[Adalynn Panascria|Adalynn]] realized that Selance Alkoth was her father, meant that the two were related. Knowing that Adalynn was his granddaughter helped push Karmagen through the last stages of The Makeven's mission.
### Life After Being a Makeven
After defeating The Casualty, Karmagen was filled with an odd sense of melancholy. He realized the effects of a missing father could do to a family, even if it wasn't his fault. Karmagen opened up an orphanage in [[Avalon]], inspired not only to give a family to those who may need one, but to teach them the arts of the Arcane.
