Oralla Panascira was a loving and caring professor at [[The College of Apallyon]]. She mostly taught subjects that focused on enchantments, conjurations, and charms. She is also the biological mother of [[Adalynn Panascria|Adalynn]].
### Conception of Adalynn
In the Autumn of 1040, when Panascira was 25, she had found love. A charming half-elf by the name Ruben Galloson had courted her during a Hallowsoul party. She began to see him often, and one night after a long hike through a nearby forest, he invited her back to his abode. Infatuated, Panascira agreed, and followed him home. As the sun set, he asked her if she wanted to join him in bed, and Panascira politely declined, wanting to wait until they were further in their relationship. After Galloson asked again, attempting to convince her, Panascira felt uncomfortable and attempted to leave. 

That is when the disguise dropped, as Galloson's face melted off as if it were hot wax, and [[Selance Alkoth]] revealed himself, forcing himself onto her. After he had impregnated her, Selance Alkoth disappeared into the night, and Panascira was found bound in the house two days later, as guards came to inspect Galloson's house. Panascira felt bittersweet when she was told that Ruben Galloson's body was found in the forest they had their last date in; his neck was slit, and he was left to die. There was a little solace in knowing that the Galloson she loved was real, but the trauma of that night was a heavy burden on her soul.
### Surrendering Sonomi
Once Panascira had given birth to [[Adalynn Panascria|Sonomi Madeline Panascira]] on Eilsca 58th, 1041, she had made a vital decision. The trauma of Selance Alkoth's actions that night never left her mind, and felt intentional, as if he was planning something. Panascira surrendered her child to an old friend, Raiine Rowe, in order to keep her safe. 

When Sonomi did come back to the college due to her innate nature to seek magic, Panascira had wiped her memories, and made sure that she was simply a professor, or at best a tutor to her. She didn't want her child hurt due to an evil that existed outside of her power. 
### Death
In The Makeven's final fight against The Casualty, Oralla was gravely injured, falling off the side of the cloudsail. In that moment, Adalynn knew the fight needed her, and instead signaled Yohrn to go after her. Unfortuantely, she has died long before Yohrn caught up to her.
