Selance Alkoth (born 6/13/1013) was an immensely powerful mage that was heavily involved in many historical events within Sanctuary's 11th century. 

### Early Life
Selance Alkoth, originally Lucian Gilith, was born in northern Hauvi on Brisca 13th, 1013. He was born to Tirich Gilith, known better as [[Karmagen]], who vanished when he was seven years old.

### Black Boxes
Selance Alkoth created five 'Black Boxes', which served as a sort of manifesto. They cover his life events up until 1062, portrayed in his perspective. These Black Boxes were found by [[The Makevens]] in session 23 of *Crack in Sanctuary*.
#### Box 1
"These black boxes serve the purpose of retelling my life’s events, in the… unlikely case of my untimely death, more-or-less if I lose control.
It all started when I was born, like others, but to a small village on the now taken peninsula of Hauvi. Specifically taken by Hauvi after the war ended, almost as an incentive by Bivouula. 
But that doesn’t matter.
I grew up there until I was seven, when my own mother grew sick after giving birth to her second son. My father seemed desperate to find a cure, and went on several trips to Bivoulla. Once she had died, he never returned. He wasn’t even there when she died, or for the funeral. We sent letters but never got one in return. Vanished is a good way to put it.
Certainly, I thought, he got lost. Perhaps he was attacked.
What a fool I was.
By the time I had matured, I felt the seed of hatred blossom in me. I yearned then more than ever to find him. Not for sentimental reasons, but with a murderous intent. 
I doubt he had been looking for medicine, and I told myself, I will kill him, not for abandoning me or my brother, but my mother.
I searched for answers, especially in arcana. I needed a way to find him, and I knew he was out there. I practiced magic and in that pursuit, found legends and artifacts of the past.
Soon, my hatred for my father quickly descended on my priority list, and would be on hold for obvious reasons."
#### Box 2
"In my own adventures, I had created relations with powerful figures, one of which was Texiontal. He had been eager to improve upon his body, and I was eager to practice on his body. In order to use materials from one body to improve on another, I thought, the flesh from both bodies needed to be symbiotic, so one flesh didn’t destroy the other. 
Flesh doesn’t care about relations, politics, or power, it only sees what is an invader and what is itself.
So, the flesh must be of itself; else the experiment would fail. Not only that, but in order to upgrade Texiontal and his body, the flesh used would have to be inherently powerful; in arcane of course.
Texiontal had offered his own twin sister, as I would be the father of this child to be used. It was later born, at the cost of the mother’s life. The baby never cried. The experiment, so far, had worked, 
The child, born as a female tiefling, was raised until she was eight years old. The child had an incredibly gifted mind, and that might have been why the experiment had gone awry. 
When I prepared the ritual, the child did not fight, despite the fact that she knew exactly what was going to happen. It was clear to me in her eyes."
#### Box 3
"While I spent my time with Texiontal, I was blind and ignorant to the world around me. Time fell through my fingers, like a desert’s sand. Unfortunately, a message being carried by a crow rather than a pigeon found my way. Several years after the event, I had been informed that my brother, Ledger, had died in the late 1020’s. He had been investigating the runes in Predoryia, that much I knew, but was killed? I came to the grim realization that I had no family left. After further investigation, I had learned that Tortle guards within the country had mercilessly killed Ledger, and it infuriated me. 
While the child grew older, I spent my time studying the Tortles and the culture of Predoryia. I shall exact my revenge on them after this project is finished."
#### Box 4
"In 1049, during the midst of my ritual, the two began to fuse, as expected. What was not expected, however, is that Texiontal was being used as the ‘material’. The child, who had been preparing for this moment, started eating away at his soul, ripping it out of the new body. 
Instead of Texiontal taking the new body and ridding the child’s soul into Eythera, the child had rid Texiontal to Hell.
As the ritual ended, the child spoke through the newly fused body, and named itself Malthu. 
Past that, I blacked out, and awoke the next day. It was to my surprise that every Orc within the immediate area was slain by “Malthu”. I had held off on finding Malthu, and counted my blessings that I somehow survived. I had other business to attend to in Predoryia that is, and after discovering a certain, special Ramon, I could finally enact my plan."
#### Box 5
"As I lured Ramon out of Predoryia, I could start killing them off one by one, until I had felt they had the same grief I have over my loss. I knew it would be a long and lengthy project, so I had continued to study self-replication and the exact study of the Arcane. 
I had looked for a suitable partner to procure powerful kin with, and it was difficult. The Lancards stuck out like a sore thumb, but they were untouchable, even to me. Another woman, by the name of Oralla Panascira, caught my interest."
