Texiontal was an Orcish warlord that controlled much of [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|Ilkon]]’s [[Son Island]] from 997 until his demise in 1049. 
### Early Life
He was born in 978 to parents that he killed shortly after his sixth birthday. When he turned 13, Texiontal joined his clan’s war efforts in a turf war against a neighboring clan, as he was promised a full pardon for his double homicide. After fighting valiantly for two years, Texiontal concocted a plan to gain power within his clan. In the name of ‘honor’, he turned the clan against their elders, killing them all, as Texiontal became the clans new sole leader, killing anyone who questioned him.
By the time he turned 19, Texiontal had either slain or absorbed the majority of Orcish tribes within eastern Son Island.

In an attempt to take advantage of the Bivou'rian War, he often made invasions into southern Hauvi and central Ilkon, wishing to claim more territory for the Orcish tribes of Ilkon. These invasions were a large portion why the Bivou’rian War extended past the turn of the millennium, as Hauvi and Ilkon demanded each other to deal with these Orcs. 
Despite his invasions being fruitless and offering no newly acquired land, he still enjoyed the conquest of it. 
### Creation of a Self-Successor
In 1033, he realized that his body was deteriorating as he aged, and enlisted the help of [[Selance Alkoth]] to give him a new, rejuvenated body. This ultimately led to the birth of [[The Casualty (Malthu)]] and his death in 1049.

Not more than a year after Texiontal’s death, the 167-Year War had ended.
