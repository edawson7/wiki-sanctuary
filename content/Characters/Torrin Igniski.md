Torrin Igniski is a historically significant Purple Dragonborn from [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|The Stellarian tribe of Ilkon]]. He is also the older brother of [[Calthor Igniski]]. 
### Life and Legacy
Torrin was born as a Red Dragonborn in 1035, two years prior to his younger brother. He was strong, and often involved himself in militaristic efforts, becoming a sort of folk hero along those stationed along The Stellarian borders. 

In 1062, as the Feynarians in Ilkon grew into a considerably dangerous force, Torrin was one of the first to propose the idea of an alliance with The Shade. This idea of coming together to fight a common enemy not only opened his heart up to coexisting with The Shade, but further altered his ideologies in his own faith, causing him to undergo [[Ideochromia (Magical Phenomenon)|ideochromia]], his scales shifting into a distinct Tyrian purple hue. 

Torrin later died in the war effort against The Feynarians. His sacrifice was not in vein however, as the Feynarians were massively subdued.
