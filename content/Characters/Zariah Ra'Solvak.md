Princess Zariah Ra'Solvak, born Brisca 17th, 999, is the Elven daughter and of the late King Escathier VIII, royalty of [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla]].

### Early Life
Zariah was the second and last child born to King Escathier VIII, and was born five years after her older brother Xavier. This gap in age made the relationship between the two unique. In the early years of life, the two were very close. They would wrestle and play sports, all against the wishes of the late King. 

Once Xavier reached his 30's, King Escathier began lessons and teachings in politics, readying his son for the throne. Zariah was broken down by her father, and pressed into a mold she didn't quite fit, to become the ideal princess. The next ten years of her life were a struggle. There were several acts of rebellion, time of imprisonment in her own room, and massive fights between herself and her father. Xavier separated himself from Zariah in these times to distinct himself, he didn't wish to be seen as rebellious or unruly by sheer association. 

In 1034, King Escathier VIII successfully ended the 167-Year War by acting as a mediator for [[The Republic of Hauvi|Hauvi]] and [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|Ilkon]] in working out a truce. Of course, neither side wished to compromise for the other, so Bivoulla had to make sacrifices to create an illusion of compromise for the truce. One of these sacrifices was Zariah herself, as she was offered as a wife for the prince of Ilkon. 
### Married Life
Zariah was infuriated by her father's choice, but realized he didn't have a choice. She didn't hold it against him for too long, but would not look at him during her wedding with [[Prince Gretsor Ilkon-sin]]. She attempted to act as a perfect wife for the Dragonborn prince, and it was okay for a while. Gretsor became king when his father, King Rolth Ilkon-sin III, passed in 1047. His attitude and demeanor unveiled a previously well hidden side of himself that was ugly and evil. He frequently beat Zariah, verbally abused her, and blamed her for his shortcomings. Zariah would never fight back, and instead waited for an opportune moment to strike. She trained her mental fortitude, taming her emotions and honing her body in the process.

While on tour in Hauvi in 1049, Zariah killed her husband as they went to bed. She was strategic and methodological, making it seem as if it was done by an assassin, staging a struggle, and leaving a scene that suggested she was kidnapped. Immediately, tensions grew once again between Ilkon and Hauvi, now with Bivoulla’s King at Ilkon’s side. Hauvi quickly began a search party for Zariah, and could find nothing. 

Met with the lack of any leads, and desperate to avoid a new war they would surely lose, Hauvi took a suspect and framed them. The suspect was a Neklion by the name Igra Thornton, someone who worked at the inn where Gretsor and Zariah had stayed. She was interrogated, and despite the lead investigator stating he found no motive in her, authorities lied about evidence. A planted knife that fit the description, paid coworkers that claimed she partook in anti-Ilkon speech. Search parties for Zariah were even called off when Hauvi’s leader stated that Igra claimed to incinerate the body, throwing any remnants or possessions at sea. The trial was swift and final, as Hauvi publicly executed her three weeks after King Gretsor’s death and Zariah’s disappearance.  

Before Igra was put on trial, Zariah was already on a ship to Predoryia, where she would build a false identity and train, determined to build a new life for herself.
