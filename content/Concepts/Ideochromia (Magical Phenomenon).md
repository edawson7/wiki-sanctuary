Ideochromia is a magical phenomenon that occurs within Dragonborn when their ideologies and beliefs shift drastically or indomitably. This extreme shift in their soul directly affects their physical body, most notably changing the color of their scales and skin. Historic examples of this phenomenon are [[Calthor Igniski|Calthor]] and [[Torrin Igniski]], along with the rise of Feynaria's Dragonborn cult in 1062.
