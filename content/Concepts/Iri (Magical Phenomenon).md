Iri, sometimes called The Iri, are luminous white spots that appear in a person's irises. Iri most always appear in the eyes of those that are proficient in magical arts, and may also be attributed to genetics, as offspring from those with Iri commonly also have Iri from birth, or develop it later in life. For example, direct descendants of [[The Banished]], like the [[Lancards]], most always have Iri.

The number of Iri present in one's eyes is believed to correspond to their innate arcane power; generally, the more Iri one possesses, the more proficient they are in magical arts.
