Soulrot is a magical disease of unknown origins. It is a disease of the soul, rather than one of the body, meaning that there could be no physical way to cure it (e.g. even creating a clone to transfer one's soul to in attempt to rid oneself of soulrot would be futile).

The only known survivor of soulrot is [[Karmagen]]. 
### Causes and Prevention
Although the cause of soulrot is unknown, reducing the risk of contraction is possible and easy for most people. Listed are a few high-risk activities that should be avoided, as contraction of soul rot is known.
- Studying or tampering with magical artifacts, particularly ancient artifacts
- Purchasing questionably magical cards
- Consuming suspicious magical soup
- Encountering aberrations
- Planar travel to the Shadowfell
- Meeting and/or conversing with infernal beings
- Conversing with dubious or questionable entities
- Making deals with dubious or questionable entities
- Breaking deals made with dubious or questionable entities
### Symptoms
The symptoms of soulrot are difficult to distinguish, and vary from person to person, but are nearly always the following:
- Severe fatigue
- Personality changes
- Mood swings
- Short-term memory loss
- Long-term memory loss
- Hallucinations
- Delirium
- Ataxia (sensory and cerebellar)
- Agnosia (facial, auditory and sensory)
- Changes in skin, such as paling of the skin
- Changes in the eyes, such as paling of the iris
- Weight changes, including unintended weight loss
- Lack of dreams
### Prognosis
Soulrot is always fatal. The terminal prognosis for someone infected with soulrot is typically anywhere from a couple months to a year, depending on several factors (e.g. age, eudaimonia, knowledge, and dispositional optimism). Older or pessimistic people afflicted with soulrot often have shorter prognoses, whereas someone with unyielding optimism or a vast amount of knowledge may live for two to three years with soulrot. It is believed that this is the case because the more joy or wisdom one has means there is more soul for the soulrot to consume; it simply takes a longer time. 

When soulrot reaches its fastigial stage, it finishes consuming the soul, and the host dies as a consequence. A creature with soulrot may not experience an afterlife, as its soul is utterly destroyed. 

It is possible to keep the host's body alive after their soul is consumed, but the body will remain in a comatose state until another soul occupies it.
