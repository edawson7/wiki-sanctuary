Soliana is the Goddess of the Sun and Stars in Sanctuary. She also has domain over Light, Heat, and Fire. She is the second-most worshipped deity across Sanctuary, with the majority being in [[Bivou'ra]], concentrated across both [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla|Bivoulla]] and [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|Ilkon]]. 

Soliana takes the guise of a beautiful woman with four rays that shoot from her crown, piercing through her long wavy hair. Her eyes are blinding white with no visible pupils. Symmetrical lines, like engravings, can be seen across the bridge of her nose leading through her cheekbones. Another set leads from the the corners of her mouth straight down to the base of her chin, and one, thicker line can be seen wrapping around her neck. She sports a ear-to-nose chain, and wears silk robes.
### History
According to legend, Soliana was born to a dark void, and she wandered the sky alone, nobody else in sight. It is said her footprints in the sky are the stars and Aurora Borealis is the echo of her shining voice. Soliana then found something within the world, and she absorbed it, becoming even brighter, starting to bring the sky to life, giving a bright yellow color to the sky, and creating day. From then forth, she became Soliana, Goddess of the Sun.

In truth, Soliana, as part of the Four Originalities, was created as part of [[The Pantheon Project]]. Thus, she is made nearly entirely of [[Nazcalt]].
