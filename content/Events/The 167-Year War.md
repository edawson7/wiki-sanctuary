The 167-Year War was a continental-wide war in [[Bivou'ra]]. It waged from 869 until 1035, when [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla]] organized a treaty to cease the war. The war involved [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla|Bivoulla]], [[The Republic of Hauvi|Hauvi]], and [[The Chiefdom of Ilkon|Ilkon]], but Bivoulla withdrew a majority of their forces by 962. 
### Course of The War
#### Background
Each country in Bivou'ra was established after the end of the Century of Wars in 756, but personal gripes were not completely resolved when the countries separated. Notably, both Hauvi and Ilkon believed their territories were too small, and wished to push their borders further into Bivoulla. These grudges were never let go, and over the next century accumulated into the topic of political endeavors on all sides. 
#### War Breaks Out (869)
In Eilsca of 869, Hauvian soldiers were sent to [[Son Island]] and instructed to occupy it. The island was deliberated to be part of Ilkon as per the treaty at the end of the Century of Wars, but many Orcs in Hauvi were not satisfied with this decision. The Hauvian government believed this to be an easy operation, as the island was mostly populated with The Ilrave, an Orcish tribe. This was mostly true, as The Ilrave were willing to pay taxes to the mostly Orcish soldiers sent to them. 

When tax collectors from the Council of Clans arrived in Ilvoz to retrieve taxes from The Ilrave, they were astonished when The Ilrave refused, stating they already paid taxes. Ilkon then sent The Stellarians in to Son Island to drive out the Hauvian soldiers, requesting Bivoulla for backup. The Kingdom of Bivoulla refused to intervene as they did not want to involve themselves in a conflict that doesn't directly involve them. 

The conflict on Son Island was mostly bloodless and Ilkon were intentional on only instigating Hauvian soldiers, leaving The Ilrave alone until a resolution in this conflict was found. One day, in Brosca of the same year, some Hauvian soldiers disguised themselves as Ilrave denizens, only to ambush a group of Stellarian soldiers. That day, Brosca 34th of 869, is widely considered to be the beginning of the 167-Year War. 
#### The Involvement of Bivoulla (876-879)
In the winter of 876, the Orcs of The Ilrave and Hauvi realized that this war effort was fruitless, and would eventually cause the decimation of their people. Instead of fighting each other in an endless war, the Orcs of The Ilrave and Hauvi banded together, refusing to fight each other. Eventually, both Hauvi and Ilkon ceased their efforts on Son Island, and instead launched attacks on the coast of the Silvereye Bay, often crossing into Bivoulla's territory. 

After months of fighting, many Ilkonian soldier would stay and rest in the Bivoullan city of Argentia between battles. The common opinion in Ilkon believed that Bivoulla was craven for not holding up their end of the deal, or for supporting Ilkon in the war for Son Island, and thus lead to harrassment to the locals of Argentia. One night in Welsca of 879, the locals stood up to the Ilkonian soldiers, which lead to a Red Dragonborn attacking a Half-Elven bartender. At that moment, the citizens of Argentia began attacking the soldiers in a fit of vengeance.

Unbeknownst to both the citizens of Argentia and the soldiers from Ilkon, Hauvian spies and soldiers were stationed in the city, scouting and listening for sensitive information the drunken Ilkonian soldiers may share aloud. When the battle between the soldiers and the locals began, the Hauvian infiltrators revealed themselves, attacking the Ilkonian soldiers, assisting any local residents. 

Some Ilkonian soldiers escaped, and shared a message that Bivoulla and Hauvi have allied against their country. The Kingdom of Bivoulla, outraged that their denizens were attacked by Ilkonian soldiers, the King sent for several fleets of soldiers to push any Ilkonian force back to their country. Bivoulla was still confused on why Hauvian troops were in Argentia as well, but thought better than to make two enemies at once, and entered an unspoken alliance with them. 
### Aftermath
Since the end of the 167-Year War, relations between all three countries have been amicable, as no further conflicts between countries have happened. Ilkon retained control over Son Island, as per the treaty, and Hauvi was given the Lobclaw Peninsula in what was northeastern Bivoulla.
