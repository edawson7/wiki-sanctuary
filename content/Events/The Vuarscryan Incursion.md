The Vuarscryan Incursion was an [[Incursion (Magical Phenomenon)|Incursion]] of realities that afflicted strictly the greater area of [[Vuarscrya]] in 1062 when its [[The Lanterns of Sanctuary|Lantern]] was destroyed by [[The Casualty (Malthu)|Malthu]] of [[The Rightful Valor]].

In the Lantern's last moments after the Incursion occurred, it thrusted the continent thousands of feet into the sky, supported by shock-solidified magma that turned into a solid obsidian pillar that spanned the width of the continent. 

As The Makevens were returning from Kalvera, they asked their newfound ally [[Elias the Elite of the Earth]] to shift the continent back to its normal elevation. He agreed, and over the course of about 15 minutes, Vuarscrya was mostly returned to its rightful elevation. Elias then returned to the elemental Plane of Earth, and The Makevens earned the credit of saving Vuarscrya from a terrible winter.

Despite this, the effects of the Incursion remained, as some still find Alternates roaming, and collisions are still reported, as dozens of people go missing each year.
