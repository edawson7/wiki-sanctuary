### The Four Originalities
These deities were created by [[Masonic Vannakah|Vannakah]] for [[The Pantheon Project]]. As such, they are made completely of [[Nazcalt]], and their relationship to life itself is questionable.
- [[Rowan|Rowan, the God of Thought and Wisdom]]
- [[Hollusk|Hollusk, the God of the Night, the Moon, and the Ring]]
- [[Soliana|Soliana, the Goddess of the Sun and the Stars]]
- [[Vahra|Vahra, the Goddess of Nature and Life]]
### Stray Gods
Stray Gods are beings of immense power that seem to appear without rhyme or reason. Their powers and influence in the world is scattered, but very real.
- [[Arenica|Arenica, the Goddess of Love and Sacrifice]]
- [[Expelias|Expelias, the God of Travel and Exploration]]
- [[The Dreamweaver|The Dreamweaver, the Goddess of Dreams and Fate]]
- [[Rennex|Rennex, the God of Luck and Destiny]]
- [[Sou'Lanter|Sou'Lanter, the God of Trickery and Vengeance]]
- [[Goddess of the Wind and Sky]]
### Dead Gods
As the name implies, these deities are considered either dying or completely eradicated. Dead deities may still be worshipped, and such worship could bring a dying God back to life, or call upon a successor, but if a God is truly departed, nothing may happen.
