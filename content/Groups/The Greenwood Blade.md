The Greenwood Blade was a group of adventuring mercenaries created and led by [[Crimwood Holo]] in 1035. The group was most active during the years 1036 through 1049, when Crimwood Holo's family has been killed.
### Members of The Greenwood Blade
- Crimwood Holo (founder, leader)
- [[Everwood Holo]] (co-founder, officially resigned in 1041)
- Renitt Moluch (dwarfish champion brawler)
- [[Artsek Hedrec]] (on occasion)
### Initiation
Crimwood Holo created The Greenwood Blade in 1035 with his younger brother Everwood Holo. The group was based in [[Rolsa, Ausonate|Rolsa]], as it was the closest largest city near [[Rowehallow]], where the two lived. They would often take the 50 mile trip between their home and Rolsa on each week, travelling a day before camping, and then finishing the trip the next day. 
### Disbandment
While The Greenwood Blade were off completing a contract in Ausonate's northern Pillarsent Island, Hurlek Hedrec had infiltrated his home in Rowehallow, murdering his wife and two children. When Crimwood Holo returned from his quest, he became broken, and despite the other member's wishes, could not return to The Greenwood Blade. 

blank attempted to lead the Greenwood Blade in the following months, but without Crimwood Holo's charisma and connections, the group eventually dissolved. Some members continued to be mercenaries on their own right, but The Greenwood Blade had become a concept of the past.
