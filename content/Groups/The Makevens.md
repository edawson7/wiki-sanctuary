The Makevens is a group of intrepid heroes that emerged in 1062. They were the leading force against [[The Casualty (Malthu)|The Casualty]], and were the first steps in preparing [[The Isles of Sancturn|Sanctuary]] against [[The Calamity]]. 

The name 'Makeven', a portmanteau of the words 'make' and 'even', was coined by the people of [[Vuarscrya]] when they facilitated a solution to return the continent to its correct elevation after [[The Vuarscryan Incursion]].
