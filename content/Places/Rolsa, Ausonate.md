Rolsa is the capital of [[The Kingdom of Ausonate|Ausonate]], and where the royal castle, Hailsoul Castle, resides. It is located in central Ausonate, and is on the coast of the Bay of Sonset. As such, it also acts as a major port city for the country, with ferries traveling between it and [[Avalon]] daily. 

Despite being the capital of Ausonate, Rolsa is only the largest city in the country by population. The largest city by area in Ausonate is [[Sorala Central]], encompassing over 100 square miles of land. 
### Royalty
Rolsa has been the capital of Ausonate since its founding as a kingdom. The Hailsoul Castle in Rolsa has been home to the Greolt royalty (pre-640), the Leadfinger Royalty (640-1062), and currently the Lancard royalty.
### Port and Exports
Despite not being the closest point between Apallyon and Ausonate, the main purpose for Rolsa's port is for ferries and exports. A ferry from Rolsa to Avalon usually takes two days, which is a much shorter time compared to how long it would take to travel from Rolsa to Gralton (a town located on the eastern-most point of the Acrated Peninsula), especially with the Acrated Mountains as an obstacle.

On average, around a thousand people travel to or from Rolsa's port each day by ferry, and dozens of ships carry exports from Rolsa to Avalon. A majority of exports from Ausonate are grain, vegetables, salt, limestone and gypsum.
