Talthen is the fourth planet orbiting the star Soliux (called Kalist by some descendants of [[The Banished]]). It is the largest of the system's three known habitable worlds, distinguished by its vast oceans, a single moon, and a thick planetary ring.
### Astronomical Data and Physical Characteristics 
Position from Soliux: 4th planet
Moons: one, named Vuliux
Rings: one thick ring, white in color, likely a mix of ice and rock
Composition: predominantly water, with one major landmass and a small archipelago

Talthen's moon, Vuliux, is distinctive for resembling an eyeball in appearance. It is not tidally locked — unlike most fictional moons, it rotates relative to Talthen, so its visible face changes over time. Which "face" of Vuliux is visible correlates with the season in Talthen's northern hemisphere.

### Vuliux (Moon)
Talthen's moon, Vuliux, is distinctive for resembling an eyeball in appearance. It is not tidally locked, so it rotates relative to Talthen, changing its visible face as time passes. Which "face" of Vuliux is visible correlates with the season in Talthen it is.
### Timekeeping
Talthen's calendar is based on the cycle of its moon and the seasons. 
Day: 23 hours
Week: 6 days
Month: 60 days, tied to Vuliux's cycle
Year: 360 days, made up of 6 months
### Geography
Talthen's surface is overwhelmingly ocean. Its landmasses are limited to one major continent and a small archipelago.
