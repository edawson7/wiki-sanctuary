Ilkon is a country located in the continent of [[Bivou'ra]]. It lies south of [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla]], and southwest of [[The Republic of Hauvi]]. Ilkon is made up of three main sections: the mainland, the [[Daughter Island]], and the [[Son Island]]. The capital of Ilkon is Pakeri, located in the northeast of the country's territory. Pakeri serves as a gateway city between the large portion of Ilkon that is arid desert, and the smaller northern portion that is much more temperate. As such, it is a major trade hub for the country.

### Culture and Society
Ilkon's population is mostly made up of Dragonborn, dragon-like humanoids lacking wings. These Dragonborn organize themselves into tribes that are constituent political entities, which may introduce legislature that enforce local cultural and religious tradition. As such, these tribes are centered around two major factors: religion and color. 

For a majority of Ilkon's history, it has acted as a Chiefdom, with its primary leader holding the title of Paragon. This method of government changed during the 167-Year War, as a stricter, more forceful figure of authority. In 906, a war general by the name of Rolth Pilex started this movement within his position, and eventually claimed royalty. When he did, he changed his surname to Ilkon-sin, meaning ‘For Ilkon’s Valor’. 

This form of government lasted until 1067 when King Rolth’s great grandson, King Gretsor Ilkon-sin, was exposed for his criminal behavior and evil actions to those in his life. The country revolted, and returned to working as a chiefdom with a single Paragon to delegate between each major tribe, leading the Council of Clan. The first and current Paragon since this return of chiefdom is Paragon Damien.
### Tribes of Ilkon
There are currently six major tribes within Ilkon, each worshipping a different God, and consisting of a different population. In order of most influential to least influential, the tribes are The Stellarians, The Shade, The Agsils, The Liage, The Feynarians, and the Ilrave. 
#### The Stellarians
Comprised of mostly Red Dragonborn that worship [[Soliana]], The Stellarians are known for their prideful, recalcitrant culture, and their exceedingly loyal relationship to the Bivoullan government and the Ra'Solvak royalty. The Stellarian capital of Ilkon is Pesynt, a sprawling emporium situated just a dozen or so miles inland from the coast of the Bay of Bask.

The zealous nature of The Stellarians has created a historic clash between themselves and The Shade. Though war is seldom the consequence of this friction, drastic and unfortunate crimes have been recorded between the two tribes. Despite this, in 1062, a Red Dragonborn by the name [[Torrin Igniski]] led an initiative to ally the tribe with The Shade in order to wage war against The Feynarians. Relations between the two tribes have been amicable since, despite their holy rivalry.
#### The Shade
The Shade heavily worship [[Hollusk]], the God of the Night, Moon, and Ring. They consist majorly of Blue Dragonborn, but often welcome other races. The capital of The Shade is Sano, a caravanserai outpost just east of the mainland Ilkon mountains.

The Shade's territory has historically been the most expanse out of all Ilkon tribes, often controlling the majority of mainland Ilkon, especially in recent times. After Feynaria's disappearance in 1063, The Shade's territory extended to border The Stellarian's, making up a total area of just over 168,000 square miles. 
#### The Agsils
The Agsils are a tribe comprised of White and Silver Dragonborn located in the northeast of Ilkon, housing both the large city of Dhon and the capital of Ilkon, Pakeri. The people of the Agsils worship an array of Gods, but a majority do worship [[Rowan]], the God of Thought and Wisdom. 

The Agsils control only around 17,000 square miles in Ilkon, but they do have dominion over the Pakeri Highway which stretches from Pesynt to [[Solvak]]. They tax traffic that use the Pakeri Highway, and a majority of that money goes towards its protection.

Despite its small size compared to The Stellarian or The Shade, it has a population of just over 850,000 people. A majority of the population exists within Dhon and Pakeri.
#### The Liage
The Liage is a tribe that mostly inhabits the Verthan Peninsula in northeast Ilkon. The Verthan Peninsula is humid and is home to many jungles and forests, which attracted an ancient group of druids hundreds of years ago that are still very present within The Liage. They make up a majority of the tribe's population, which is made up of a diverse mix of Green Dragonborn, Black Dragonborn, Humans, Orcs, and Tieflings.

The Liage govern about 9,500 square miles and has a population of roughly 16,000 people. Most of this population is evenly spread out across the Verthan peninsula, as clans and circles typically don't have more than a couple hundred people in them. These clans and circles are respectfully distanced from each other, but are close enough to be a days or so walk away. 
#### The Feynarians
The Feynarians are a tribe, or rather a cult, of Purple Dragonborn with a most unique origin. In the late 1050's, [[Aercasa]], a Golden Dragonborn, began a cult of followers in the name of Feynaria. Charged with a sense of ethnic pride and hope for continuation, many Golden Dragonborn pledged themselves to the movement. 

As the movement escalated, many of those involved underwent [[Ideochromia (Magical Phenomenon)|ideochromia]], shifting into a Purple Dragonborn with a bright magenta hue. Those who did not experience the phenomena were often killed for betrayal to Feynaria. Aercasa herself never underwent ideochromia. 
#### The Ilrave
Unlike the rest of the tribes in Ilkon, The Ilrave is an Orcish tribe located on Son Island. The Ilrave was a unified tribe that originated from the southern stretches of Hauvi, until in 1049 when [[The Casualty (Malthu)|Malthu]] wiped over 9,200 Orcs across the island before founding [[The Rightful Valor]]. 

An estimated 2,000 Orcs were left alive in Malthu's warpath, and The Ilrave was essentially dismantled into several other tribes. These remaining tribes wage war against one another, each claiming to be the true successor to The Ilrave. Due to this everchanging field of power, the collection of Orcish tribes in Ilkon is often simply referred to The Ilrave. 
#### The Auruics
The Auruics are a dead tribe that was made up of Golden Dragonborn, mostly located on the Sister Island and reaching up into the southern reaches of Ilkon's mainland. Through the mid 900's through the early 1000's, the tribe experienced an extended period of infertility and illness. In the 1020's, the population was under a thousand.

In 1062, the last known Golden Dragonborn, Aercasa, was killed by [[The Makevens]]. The Golden Dragonborn race, and by extension the Auruic tribe, is now considered extinct.
