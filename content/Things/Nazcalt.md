Nazcalt is a metal alloy that was introduced to the people of [[Taltyro]] by [[The Intruder]]. The incredibly dense and magically synchronized alloy is best known for its usage in the creations of both [[The Lanterns of Sanctuary|The Lanterns]] and the Gods created by [[Masonic Vannakah]] in [[The Pantheon Project]]. 
### Properties
Nazcalt is characterized by its dark, desaturated blue-violet coloration, often described as 'charcoal blue'. It has a density of approximately 41.9 g/cm³ and a melting point of nearly 4,600 °F (2,538 °C). The alloy is notably soft for a metal, a property that makes it well suited to etching and carving, particularly for the inscription of sigils and runes. Despite its softness, Nazcalt exhibits strong corrosion resistance, remaining inert in the presence of both water and oxygen; it is vulnerable, however, to degradation by strong acids. Electrically, Nazcalt is highly resistive, dissipating large electrical currents as heat rather than conducting them. 

Its most distinctive property is an exceptional affinity for arcane magic. Sigils and runes carved into its surface function as a form of physical programming, and once inscribed, the resulting configuration becomes fixed, resistant to alteration by external magical influence and modifiable only through direct physical means. 
### Origins
Nazcalt is not native to [[Talthen]], but was brought to world by The Intruder. As a result, the methods and components required to produce Nazcalt remain unknown, and no means of replication has been discovered. Following The Intruder's disappearance, no further source of the alloy has emerged, leaving the existing supply of Nazcalt on Talthen extremely finite. The total quantity of Nazcalt across Talthen is estimated at approximately 4,000 cubic meters; this figure does not account for losses incurred during smelting, working, or other processing, such as material lost as slag.
