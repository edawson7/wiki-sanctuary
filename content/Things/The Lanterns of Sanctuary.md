The Lanterns of Sanctuary were hidden generators placed around [[The Isles of Sancturn]] to create a sanctuary against [[The Calamity]]. They were invented and constructed by [[Masonic Vannakah]] of [[The Syndicate]] in hopes of giving the last bastion against The Calamity a chance to create a force that may be able to defeat The Calamity. 
### History
#### Conceptualization
Even with the four weapons of the Pantheon Project at their side, the war was an uphill battle. The Syndicate began to evacuate people to the Isles of Sancturn, a relatively untouched cluster of continents to the west of [[Taltyro]].

In the coming months, there was a moment of respite for the survivors of The Calamity's war. Vannakah, with the rest of The Syndicate, worked hard to begin erecting defenses for when The Calamity would finally come attack. Vannakah, with roughly 200 cubic meters of [[Nazcalt]], began drafting designs of generators that would create a sort of forcefield around the Isles of Sancturn. 

Vannakah had landed on a design that resembled a street lantern, standing at about 8 feet tall. These generators would be hidden around the Isles of Sancturn, one to each major continent, and would be connected together through arcane means. Made nearly entirely of Nazcalt and planned to be charged with the souls of powerful mages, these lanterns were inscribed with intricate runes that programmed their purposes. The first and primary purpose was to shield the Isles of Sancturn from outside forces. The second purpose was to affect the flow of time for the world outside of their sanctuary, halting any progress made by The Calamity. This, he hoped, would present the bastion of Sanctuary the opportunity to advance and prepare an army capable of destroying The Calamity.
#### Implementation
Vannakah had sent [[Elecast Kahral]], [[Ramon Kamua Kia’i]], and [[Bella Anthenium]] to scout for discreet locations to place the lanterns. In [[Bivou'ra]], the lantern was placed deep inside a volcanic vent in Mount Caluminin, an active volcano in modern-day [[The Kingdom of Bivoulla|Bivoulla]]. In [[Vuarscrya]], a deep cave within [[The Kingdom of Apallyon|Apallyon's]] Avlanti mountain range was chosen, and a deadly dungeon was built around it to ensure nobody would pursue further. In [[Predoryia]], Ramon chose a cave below Muamuakai to place the lantern, and covered the walls of the cave with magical warding runes. The lantern placed in [[Efox]] was hidden similarly to the lantern of Vuarscyra, near Paradin Cove. 

During the implementation of the lanterns, Vannakah discovered an unintended side effect. While testing one of the lanterns, he found that his recent memories had been erased and that the lantern had remained powered far longer than expected. The charge he had supplied was calculated to last approximately five minutes, but the lantern continued operating for nearly two hours. Because he had been keeping written notes, he was able to reconstruct what had occurred: he had lost roughly two days' worth of memories.

Further study confirmed that memory consumption, rather than the intended energy source being souls of the magically inclined, was extremely effective for charging the lanterns. This meant the lanterns no longer required a sacrifice of life to function; instead, the sacrifice would be memories, though the amount lost could not be controlled or predicted.

After a week of deliberation, the Syndicate developed [[The Mementome]] in preparation of the new method of powering the lanterns. The change eliminated the need for mages to sacrifice their lives and extended the lanterns' estimated lifespan from a few centuries to at least two millennia. The Syndicate then activated all of the lanterns simultaneously, marking the founding of Sanctuary.
#### Termination of The Lanterns
1062 years after the establishment of the Lanterns, [[The Casualty (Malthu)|Malthu]] of [[The Rightful Valor]] had successfully destroyed the Lantern of Vuarscrya, despite the efforts of The [[The Makevens]]. When it cracked and broke apart, the fabric of reality was torn asunder for a moment, causing what is known now as the [[The Vuarscryan Incursion|Incursion of Vuarscrya]]. In the Lantern's last moments of cognizance, it focused on two things. First, it thrusted the continent of Vuarscrya into the air as a means to reduce the effects of the [[Incursion (Magical Phenomenon)|Incursion]]. Then, the Lantern sent out a distress signal, sent directly and instantaneously to the other Lanterns.

As the other Lanterns received this signal, they instantly deliberated; not like people would, but similar to how systems or artificial intelligence might. Over the next few minutes, the Lanterns thought up thousands of hypotheticals, simulations, and possiblities of how to serve Sanctuary. In harmonious agreement, the Lanterns have made their decision and considered the Lantern of Efox to become an Avatar of the Lanterns. Before long, the Lanterns of Bivou'ra and Predoryia were destroyed, but they had already sent most of their power to Efox. It lied in wait until it was captured in order to take initiative on its own to save Sanctuary. 
### The Saint
[[The Saint]] was created as a self-conceptualization by the Lantern of Efox, and acquired a body though the possession of a stowaway child. Within a few weeks, The Saint had found The Makevens and instructed them to make a replacement lantern that would protect Sanctuary for a few more years. Once the replacement lantern was placed in the center of all four continents within the Isles of Sancturn, she inserted herself, and became the battery that powered the Lantern. For just about 25 more years, Sanctuary was protected by an ultra-powerful Lantern with The Saint inside.
