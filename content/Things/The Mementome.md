The Mementome is a record of events left behind by [[The Syndicate]], meant to be found by later generations when the time was right. As wished, it was found by [[The Makevens]] with the guidance of [[Selance Alkoth]], as he had attempted to search for it himself to no avail. 

The Makevens would find The Mementome in Bivoulla*, eventually handing it over to [[Ardwenn Yohrn|Yohrn]] at the [[The College of Apallyon]], which he kept in his personal horde of historical artifacts. The Mementome and its contents were analyzed and then partially integrated into the Panascira and Sruvak curriculums after [[The Casualty (Malthu)|The Casualty]]'s defeat in 1062.

#### Preface
Written by [[Elson Rowe|Elson V. Rowe]]

This is a Memento to remember the world of [[Merrihaven]] on the hidden gem of [[Talthen]]. As an author, I will strive to keep this tome unbiased in order to keep the knowledge of this world true and genuine. I may not live to finish this tome, but I must try. Preserving the knowledge of what is going to happen to Merrihaven’s people may help my descendants, if life on Talthen as we know it isn’t annihilated. 

Split into five books, this tome will cover as much knowledge that I have access to from the beginning of Merrihaven to the bitter end.

And so, for their crimes against the Langardians, they were one of the many groups that were sent to distant worlds where they may spread life through the universe, away from Langard.

The trip itself was dark, stressful, and violent. [[The Banished]] turned on each other in the insanity of the darkness, a miserable fight of blame, bickering, and blasphemy; a fight with no winner. Eventually, though, The Banished people ceased. Those few who have already given up hope had taken their lives, but a revelation came about the others. This could be their new start. It may not be as luxurious, as comfortable, or as civilized as Langard, but now they are no longer known as criminals to whatever world they end up in, and that was a spark of hope to most. 
After what seemed to be a decade, they arrived at their destination. A world named, from their knowledge, Talthen.

The founders of Merrihaven were Gitvor Kahral, Vonnas-Soliana, along with Miast and Nalogi-Rowe, who banded all of what is left of The Banished to one clan who sought to make this world ‘home’, make it their second chance.
##### Chapter 2: The Natives
Talthen itself had life already, and an aura that eventually was tamed, and became the cultivation of magic, thanks to research done by Brivalm Panascira. 
The life of Talthen was complicated, and seemed to exist within two worlds simultaneously. Fey were the majority of the population, along with minorities such as dragons, goblins, orcs, several birdfolk, and tortles. 

Though, saying the population is scarce is an exaggeration. Finding intelligent or semi-coherent life was a challenge. It took about six years before the banished encountered conscious life, specifically a [[Deities of Talthen|Great Faerie]]. It was Gitvor Kahral and his son, Elecast Kahral, that encountered the Great Faerie. It is said that it was a moment that lasted centuries, as Gitvor Kahral decided not to fight, not to run, but to bow down to the Great Faerie. She, named [[Kaleido|Kaleido: The Mother of the Seasonal Faeries]], saw this as an instance of respect, instead of fear or hostility, and proved to the native inhabitants of Talthen that these forlorn people are not animals, and they are not unreasonable. Unlike the other natives of Talthen, the Fey found The Banished quite interesting and intriguing.

Throughout the next century or so, through diplomatic deals, the Fey moved to make [[Kalvera (The Feywild)|Kalvera]], or what we called, the ‘Feywild’, their home. As such, the Fey left what they called their land, [[Taltyro]], to The Banished. To specify, the name of the world itself is considered Talthen, and Taltyro is the name of the continent, widely believed to be the largest continent on Talthen.
#### Book 2: The Renaissance
##### Chapter 1: The Expelian
The Banished had made Taltyro their home, while staying in consistent contact with the Fey through Merrihaven, as a place that exists on both the Kalvera and Talthen. Large cities started appearing throughout the main continent, and a second capital was made, named Siallyte. Merrihaven and Siallyte became the beacons of humanity throughout Taltyro, and thus a road was made to connect the two. It was dubbed “The Expelian”, a road where any one person could walk safely over the course of four thousand miles. Towns and Villages branched from The Expelian, as the population boomed with a sort of Golden Age.
##### Chapter 2: The Races
With the population booming throughout Taltyro, new families were being born. Some even married Fey, or other natives to Talthen. The dragons of Talthen were especially curious of The Banished, after the Fey confirmed they were not of malintentions of course.

The Magical essence of Talthen also aided with newfound relationships, or even evolution, as one's surroundings truly did change who they are. So-called Elves were a byproduct of Fey and Humans pairing, whereas Dragonborn and other Lizardfolk came about with Dragons and Tortles pairing with Humans. Dwarves began with seclusion in the mountains or mines, along with lineages of Halflings and Gnomes later down the lines.
##### Chapter 3: The Lineages
The more and more blood was spread, and recycled, the fewer and fewer of those who kept their Langardian staple; their [[Iri (Magical Phenomenon)|Iri]]. Iri, by definition, are the luminescent spots in one’s irises. This is a true sign of Langardian blood. The exact number of how many vary, but are still a clear staple of Langardian blood, no matter where they are from. 

Despite the majority of the population losing their Iri, those who kept their last name, and stayed true to their ancestry kept them. This faithfulness seemingly had a byproduct of power, in some archaic way. They are gifted. 

From the list of The Banished, the following names had so far kept their Iri: Athenium, Gilith, Kahral, Likero, Panascira, Rowe, and Vannakah.
#### Book 3: The Intrusion
##### Chapter 1: The Echo
In the morning of the 12th of Varsca, as civilization was thriving in Taltyro, an echo shot through the sky. The echo was loud enough to be heard in both Merrihaven and Siallyte. A small town named Kinecard, about an hour's walk away from The Expelian, heard it the loudest, later reporting an anomaly within their woods. A small crater made within the dirt had an odd plant placed within the mess. This plant is nowhere found on Talthen, but quickly spread. It is spiny in nature, and seems to reproduce through runners, or seed. When torn, metallic fibers can be seen, and have black veins throughout its leaves. This plant was unanimously named Blackhart. The properties of Blackhart had no use, at least to those average people.
##### Chapter 2: The Arrival
On the 32nd of Varsca, that same year, a man fell from the sky. He had introduced himself as [[The Delegate]]. Hair was shaved down on one side, and the other was quite unkept, pupils shaped like a snake’s, and odd tusk-like horns extruding from the chin and cheekbones. 

Upon arrival, The Delegate searched for a plant, specifically the Blackhart. A few lead him to a now Blackhart-polluted forest, and it had seemed that’s all he wanted to see. As he walked back, the locale offered him a feast, almost making sure we were on his good side. It seemed obvious he was powerful, and perhaps a deity.

When the Fey were questioned of who The Delegate was, they had no clues of who he may be, but were immediately sour towards him. We were cautioned to be wary.
##### Chapter 3: The Intruder
After staying for a week, The Delegate had disappeared. The locale of Kinecard wondered to themselves if he was an illusion, a devil, or a celestial. Soon enough, within the next month, The Delegate returned along with another. The figure stood shorter than The Delegate, but more toned. Dark black hair, almost accentuated with indigo, fell to a length of his upper back. Black eyes with purple irises, and two sets of eyebrows. Black markings ran along his cheekbones, and a pentacle inscribed on his forehead. This was [[The Intruder]].

At first, the people of Taltyro were against these people, as the Fey had cautioned them, and the Fey have never failed The Banished, yet a mysterious feeling flooded those around The Intruder, as he talked with the locals, and celebrated his arrival with them. He had a dark charisma about him.
##### Chapter 4: The Arcane
The Delegate and The Intruder taught lessons to those they deemed worthy of their knowledge, what they called [[Animancy|animancy]], or translated now to be the Arcane. Within the next decade, powerful users of the natural magic that lied in Talthen came about, as more and more of the population trusted these intruders more than the Fey, who had kept the Arcane to themselves. The opportunities were endless, and The Intruder was their new king.
#### Book 4: The Fall
##### Chapter 1: The War
Sometime within the next year, a natural being of Talthen was targeted by The Intruder. Several dragons were killed and destroyed, as he reportedly had taken their lives and Archaic energy. More now than ever was Taltyro split, as those worshiping the Intruder had to either realize what he had done, or live in ignorance labeled faith and start war.

Dragons, fearing this Intruder, had started to take the forms of humans. Some dragons disguised themselves and hid away, while others disguised themselves and fought alongside the Fey Followers, despite what differences they had with the Fey in the past.

From that moment forward, war and chaos broke out within Taltyro, as the Intruder and The Delegate superpowered their army to fight off the Fey Followers. The Fey helped their allies in whatever way they could, but from another world, it proved to be difficult. 
##### Chapter 2: The Syndicate
The Syndicate was founded by the recurring soul of [[Elecast Kahral]], as a sort of pact of powerful beings. Elecast had his eye on a few individuals, namely [[Masonic Vannakah]], [[Ramon Kamua Kia’i]], and myself. This Syndicate would be of complete secrecy, in order to keep any infiltrators or followers of The Intruder away. Names would be discarded, and replaced with generic titles. The Founder, The Creator, The Defender, and The Author would be the names of those who made up The Syndicate.
##### Chapter 3: The Pantheon
In the course of this war, The Creator felt this sense of incoming doom. Gifted with abilities beyond any craftsman he knows, he began planning for something he called [[The Pantheon Project]]. He drafted prints of guardians that would keep Taltyro safe, in the event of catastrophe. There was no time for prototypes, he had convinced himself. It had to be perfect, the first try no matter what.

He had named the first among the list [[Rowan]], for the Rowe lineage. Then [[Soliana]] after the Solian lineage, and [[Hollusk]] after the Hol’Dusk lineage. Finally, [[Vahra]], named after his mother. His creations would soon come to fruition.

In his studies, he declared he needed a strong yet versatile metal to construct them out of. Luckly for him, The Intruder had introduced a new metal into this world, dubbed [[Nazcalt]]. It was a metal that could be created, forged from plain materials. The harvesting process, though, was needy and complicated.

Nazcalt was found to be a lively alloy, and seemed to have a will of its own. There were methods to control this, in terms of giving a sort of instinct to it, or setting its ‘mind’ on a certain pattern.

After many months, The Creator had built Rowan, Hollusk, Soliana, and Vahra, in that order. He constructed them in such a way that they would be able to think for themselves, and possibly even be considered alive.
##### Chapter 4: The Chaos
As the world was at war, another was brought to Talthen, [[The Calamity]]. The Delegate called it an Agent of [[The Intruder|Discord]], and had let it run loose, as chaos struck all over Taltyro. People went missing, not only from the Fey Followers, but from those worshiping The Intruder as well.

The Calamity had scaly skin, four beaming eyes, and a jaw that stretched around the entire jaw.  Despite looks, The Calamity was not an animal. It had its own conscious thoughts, and meticulously killed for the purpose of gaining power. Some turned to The Calamity and created a cult, creating even more chaos within Taltyro. 

A lone warrior by the name [[Bella Anthenium]] had taken it upon herself to hunt The Calamity down, searching for an army to help her. This was the first step towards forming The Syndicate that we know today. The Founder had reached out to her, and offered her a spot within The Syndicate. She gladly accepted, and became known as The Warrior.
##### Chapter 5: The Mistake
To the surprise of everyone in The Syndicate, and to the misfortune of anyone they meant to protect, a prototype that The Creator had built for The Pantheon Project, named [[Feynaria]], was stolen by the opposing side. Most of Feynaria's parts were recycled for the other the other four, but The Calamity had refurbished Feynaria for himself, using her god-like powers to decimate points of interest for him.
##### Chapter 6: The Evacuation
The Calamity’s terror spanned across Taltyro, and a fight was out of the question for now. The Syndicate had proposed an evacuation of people to [[The Isles of Sancturn]], a recently discovered collection of landmasses that has been untouched, as The Fey told them exactly where to go. 
The Syndicate had collected over ten thousand people to send to The Isles, and within the year sent them. A second evacuation of civilians was sent, about fifteen thousand people. A third was collected, a sum of five or so thousand people, but as they were being evacuated, they were attacked by The Calamity's forces, and could not make it. In the end, Taltyro was considered lost, claimed by The Calamity and its cult.
#### Book 5: The Lanterns
##### Chapter 1: The Concept
The Creator had realized the state of Talthen, and took action. Firstly, he took what materials remained of the Pantheon Project and recycled them into usable stock. The Calamity was quickly spreading throughout the entirety of Talthen, spreading quickly to the Isles of Sancturn. 

The main concept encompassed shielding the Isles of Sancturn from The Calamity. This shield would have to last for as long as needed until The Calamity could be defeated. With this in mind, The Creator decided to make four separate generators for the shields, as if one was destroyed, the other three would still be powered, and the majority of the Isles of Sancturn would be safe. Each Isle would have a generator, placed in hidden and safe spots to make sure it could not be turned off, that is until warriors rise up against The Calamity.
##### Chapter 2: The Prototype
Prints were drafted and thrown around, as The Creator struggled to make a design with the pressure falling on him. The material must be of Nazcalt, similarly to those creations in the Pantheon Project, just to make sure they can last. Eventually, a prototype was created, and when activated created a large archaic shockwave throughout the Isles of Sancturn. Temporarily, the everso powerful chaos of The Calamity had ceased, but resumed. As a side effect, memories were erased for a second, and came back quickly. This side effect was a worry at first, but The Creator found use for it.

The Creator had realized he found the correct algorithm and equilibrium within this generator. With the time left before The Isles were completely consumed, this generator must be used, and so it was repaired with the Nazcalt and used as a model for the next three. 

Resourcing all of the Nazcalt was difficult, and with help of The Founder, a group of people were brought together to assist with harvesting Nazcalt, and forging it into three more Lanterns, as they were dubbed by The Defender.
##### Chapter 3: The Placement
As the Lanterns were now created, placement was key. In fact, I, The Author, am restricted from recording the selected areas in this Mementome, but can write the process. The Creator had the long run in mind, making sure that looters and those with malintentions lack any ability to find them, and embraced the side effect of the memory erasure to make sure nobody knew where the locations of the Lanterns were.

Placing the Lanterns among The Isles was somewhat tricky, as there needed to be one for each, and placed in a hidden spot. Sailors volunteered to assist in the placement, and The Syndicate was split, as one or two went to each Isle to guide the sailors. The Creator went to [[Efox]], as he wanted to make certain the most important Lantern was safe. The Defender went to [[Predoryia]], as The Founder and I went to [[Vuarscrya]], and The Warrior to [[Bivou'ra]].
##### Chapter 4: The Sanctuary
The Initiation of the Lanterns will theoretically cause the population of the Isles to forget their history, including The Syndicate, The Calamity, and so forth. The Isles of Sancturn will become a sort of Sanctuary, with the Pantheon looking over them, as Rowan, Hollusk, and Soliana will be activated.

Life will have a chance to prepare and grow before being faced with The Calamity, unless of course the Lanterns are found and broken. As The Author, I can only hope that this Mementome is found at the right time at some point in the future, so that a resistance can be made for when The Calamity reawakens.
